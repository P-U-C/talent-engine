# Prezenti AI Builder Sponsorships (Trial)

**Ask:** $7,000 from redeployed funds already held by Prezenti; no new treasury
request.

**Duration:** four months.

**Recipients:** five individual builders.

## Purpose

Prezenti will sponsor best-in-class AI coding tools for five high-agency
builders for whom the cost is a genuine access barrier. The aim is not to fund
people already circulating inside Celo. It is to identify strong blockchain and
agent-infrastructure builders from outside the ecosystem, remove a practical
constraint, and give them a focused route to ship useful, potentially
revenue-producing products on Celo.

Each recipient may claim:

- Claude Max 20x at up to $200 per month for four months;
- ChatGPT Pro at up to $100 per month for four months; and
- a $200 flexible allowance across the term.

The maximum support is $1,400 per recipient, reimbursed against monthly
receipts. Before Prezenti pays, finalists will be routed where eligible through
Claude for Open Source and OpenAI's
[Codex for Open Source](https://developers.openai.com/community/codex-for-oss/)
program. Any subscription obtained without cost reduces the reimbursement and
returns the unused amount to the Strategic Reserve.

## Selection

Applications are evaluated by the published open-source talent engine. Its
100-point technical score measures the preceding six months of public work:

| dimension | points |
|---|---:|
| shipped original work | 40 |
| sustained cadence | 20 |
| external validation | 10 |
| collaboration and review | 5 |
| blockchain ecosystem footprint | 10 |
| emerging agent-protocol signal | 15 |

Stars, followers, credentials, raw commit totals and the proposed build plan do
not score. The taxonomy covers blockchain broadly and gives additional weight
to MCP, ERC-8004, x402, A2A and related agent infrastructure. Existing Celo
history is useful but not required.

The score produces an evidence-linked shortlist; it does not make the final
funding decision. Prezenti will shortlist approximately fifteen applicants for:

1. verification of the underlying repositories and external contributions;
2. confirmation that the subscription cost is a meaningful access barrier;
3. review of a credible four-month build plan with a Celo deployment,
   integration or material ecosystem contribution;
4. review of authenticity flags and relationships between applicant accounts;
5. conflicts review and final steward judgment.

Celo regional scouts will proactively refer builders from their communities.
An endorsement is sourcing and review context rather than technical points
until the engine has a referral mechanism that proves who made it. Selected
dossiers and decision rationales will be published with recipient consent.

## Recipient terms

- Subscriptions are personal and non-transferable and tied to the recipient's
  named GitHub identity.
- Reimbursements require monthly receipts and evidence of continuing activity.
- Each recipient posts one monthly build-in-public update to a shared forum
  thread.
- Thirty days without qualifying project activity triggers review. The
  recipient has seven days to provide evidence or explain the interruption
  before remaining support may be cancelled.
- Authenticity flags trigger manual review. Confirmed material
  misrepresentation may void the sponsorship; a metric flag alone does not.
- Continued support for months three and four requires a verifiable Celo
  mainnet deployment, integration or material ecosystem contribution by the
  month-two checkpoint.
- The term is fixed at four months with no automatic renewal. Unused or offset
  funds return to the Strategic Reserve.

## Public give-back pledge

Each recipient makes a public 2% good-faith give-back pledge covering the
applicant-controlled product identified in the sponsored build plan.

Where that product generates on-chain revenue through Celo, 1% of revenue
actually received will be routed to Prezenti and 1% to the Celo Community Fund
through a public Splits contract. Both complete recipient addresses must be
verified in the deployment configuration before a Split is created. If the
project later launches a token or
creates value through another structure, the recipient will publicly document
how they intend to honour the same 1%/1% commitment. The pledge is not equity,
a token warrant or an investment agreement.

An empty Split is not an outcome. Deployment of the give-back mechanism is
reported only when a sponsored product has revenue to route.

## Outcomes

The trial will report:

- the full funnel from scouted to contacted, applied, shortlisted, selected and
  deployed;
- at least three verified Celo mainnet deployments or material contributions;
- at least one product demonstrating external usage or an on-chain revenue
  event;
- at least 90% compliance with monthly public updates;
- at least four recipients actively building at the end of month four; and
- all subscription offsets and unused funds returned to the Reserve.
